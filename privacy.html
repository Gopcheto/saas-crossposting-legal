1. Who We Are

SaaS Crossposting is a publishing tool that lets content creators upload media once and distribute it automatically to the social and messaging platforms they connect. This policy explains what data we collect to run that service and how we handle it.

2. What We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account infoEmail, display name, hashed password
Content you uploadPhotos and videos, stored encrypted
Connected accountsOAuth tokens for platforms you link, encrypted at rest
BillingSubscription status and plan — card details are handled directly by Stripe, we never see or store full card numbers
Usage dataLogin timestamps, publishing history, error logs

3. How We Use It

  • To operate the Service — publishing your content to the platforms you select.
  • To process subscription payments and keep your account status accurate.
  • To run the automated check that keeps content compliant on platforms with stricter policies, before it's published there.
  • To detect abuse and keep the Service secure.

4. Who We Share Data With

We share the minimum data necessary with: Stripe, to process payments; the platforms you explicitly connect (Telegram, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, and others as they're added), only to publish the content you select; and our automated content-moderation provider, which analyzes a file for compliance immediately before publishing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share your private content library with anyone beyond what's needed to deliver the Service you asked for.

5. Content Encryption & Access

No human access by default

Your content is encrypted at rest. There is no screen, endpoint, or dashboard view that lets our staff open or download your private content library — only the automated publishing process can decrypt a file, and only at the moment it's needed to complete an action you requested.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your content and personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records (for example, billing records) for legal or accounting purposes.

7. Your Rights

You can access, export, or delete your data from your dashboard, or by contacting us directly. You can disconnect any connected platform at any time, which removes our access to it.

8. Cookies

We use only the minimal, strictly necessary cookies or local storage required to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Age Requirement

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this Policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed in your account dashboard.
