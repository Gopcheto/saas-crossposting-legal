Crossposting

Publish your content everywhere, automatically.

Crossposting is a tool for independent content creators who post on multiple platforms. Upload a photo or video once — from a web dashboard or a Telegram bot — and Crossposting publishes it to the social and messaging platforms you connect, on your schedule.

Upload once, publish everywhere

Send a photo or video a single time and choose which connected platforms receive it.

Works from Telegram or the web

Manage uploads and connected accounts from a bot or from your browser dashboard.

Automated compliance checks

Content is checked automatically against each platform's requirements before it's published.

You control every connection

Connect or disconnect any platform at any time. Credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest.
